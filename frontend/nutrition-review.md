## Notes to check

- **The Big One** is a 750 g beef sharing burger, so its total is very high (~3,300 kcal). This is realistic for 750 g of 80/20 beef plus six cheese layers and bacon; consider showing it per share if that suits the menu better.
- **Sauces** are listed per full 80 ml cup, which is why the aioli and secret sauce are high in fat.
- **Natural Juice, Mojito (non-alcoholic)** assumes fresh lime and mint lengthened with water and lightly sweetened. If it is made with no added sugar, it would be closer to ~20 kcal. The menu currently says "no added sugar" for all juices; please confirm the mojito recipe.
- **Bun & Patty Signature Burger** (~1,210 kcal) includes 2 fried eggs (~90 kcal each) and 2 slices of American cheese; "Extra American Cheese" is one 20 g slice.
- **Mozzarella Burger** assumes a ~110 g breaded, deep-fried mozzarella patty (~380 kcal), 30 g cheese sauce, and 20 g house special sauce.
- **Chicken Wings** base = 8 floured, fried wings (~320 g bone-in) + 30 g garlic-herb dip. The flavor choice adds the glaze: Spicy (butter-chili, ~60 kcal) or BBQ (~80 kcal, sugar-heavy). "Extra Wings (4 pcs)" is counted without extra glaze.
- **Ketchup** is a 30 ml cup (~30 kcal).
- **Coffee** values assume whole milk and no sugar unless a syrup extra is added.
- **Zero Sugar sodas** and **Water** are effectively 0–1 kcal; lemon wedges are treated as negligible.
