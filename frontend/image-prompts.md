# Food & Beverage Photography Prompts for BUN & PATTY

This document specifies the exact art direction and studio prompts used for the BUN & PATTY menu photography.

## Art Direction Guidelines
- **Perspective**: 50mm lens at f/4, camera slightly elevated above eye level (~25°), sharp foreground food with creamy background bokeh.
- **Set & Surface**: Rustic dark oiled wooden plancha serving tray with dark printed check/branding greaseproof liner.
- **Background**: Dark restaurant interior with rich deep red leather booth seating, soft ambient amber lighting, and blurred colored neon signage in the distant background.
- **Lighting**: Controlled directional studio softbox with warm golden rim light, deep charcoal shadows, and glistening appetizing highlights.
- **Textures**: Glossy butter-toasted brioche, crispy lacy Maillard smash beef edges, melting aged cheddar, crisp shredded greens, and fresh herb flecks.
- **Sauces & Drinks**: Clean studio product isolates centered on #0B1416 dark green-black background with subtle 1px border.

- Same small matte dark ceramic bowl, same angle, same warm directional softbox light with golden rim light, same dark slate surface with a softly blurred dark background in every sauce photo.
- Image fills the whole card (16:10/16:9), no empty padding or borders, no text, no logo, no branding, no hands.
